How it works in the real world

From “what is this thing?” to “we can’t run the wash without it” in about a day.

1

Mount camera & Pi

Mount an off‑the‑shelf camera at the entry or exit. Your PSI box (Raspberry Pi + screen) plugs into power and network.

2

Connect to PSI cloud

The box securely checks in, loads your site rules, and starts sending anonymized lane events to our servers.

3

Staff sees simple screens

Green = good, yellow = double‑check, red = do not send. No AI jargon, just clear “what do I do with this car?” guidance.

4

You get analytics

Re‑wash rate, incident logs, PSI suggestions, and rule overrides – all summarized in a web dashboard for managers.

Questions operators actually ask

Short answers now, deep dive on a call.

What hardware do I need?

A Raspberry Pi (we recommend Pi 5 with 8–16 GB), a decent SD/SSD, a monitor or touchscreen at the lane, and at least one camera per module (entry, exit, etc.). We’ll send a reference parts list.

Does this replace my POS, tunnel controller, or existing systems?

No. PSI sits beside them as a camera‑driven assistant. It can be integrated, but it doesn’t try to replace your core stack.

Is customer data safe?

Images and events are sent to the cloud over encrypted connections. You control what gets stored and for how long. We don’t sell or share your data with third‑party advertisers.

Can staff override decisions?

Yes. The lane UI always lets staff override with a tap, and those overrides become analytics for future training and tuning.

What if the internet goes down?

The lane box can continue making decisions using the last downloaded model and rules. It will sync logs when the connection comes back.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel a site at the end of its billing month. You still own the hardware and can repurpose it internally.
